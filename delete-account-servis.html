Delete your ULOV+ Servis account

This page explains how to delete your account and associated data from ULOV+ Servis (package uz.misterdev.ulov.admin), the staff/admin app for car service centers, published by ULOV.

How to request deletion

Send an email to ulugbek9015t@gmail.com with the subject line “Delete account” from the phone number or email registered to your account, or include your registered phone number in the message. We verify ownership and delete the account within 30 days.

What data is deleted

  • Your profile: name, phone number, email, city, profile photo.
  • Your service center profile, gallery photos and uploaded service photos.
  • Vehicles, service records and comments you created.

What may be retained

  • Payment and subscription records required for accounting / legal obligations are kept for up to 5 years, then deleted.
  • Anonymized, aggregated statistics that cannot identify you.

ULOV+ Servis akkauntini o‘chirish

Bu sahifa ULOV+ Servis ilovasidan (uz.misterdev.ulov.admin) akkauntingiz va unga bog‘liq ma’lumotlarni qanday o‘chirishni tushuntiradi.

O‘chirishni qanday so‘rash mumkin

ulugbek9015t@gmail.com manziliga “Delete account” mavzusi bilan, akkauntga ro‘yxatdan o‘tgan telefon raqamingizni ko‘rsatib email yuboring. Egaligini tasdiqlab, akkauntni 30 kun ichida o‘chiramiz.

Ilovaning profil/sozlamalar bo‘limida “Akkauntni o‘chirish” tugmasi ham mavjud.

Qanday ma’lumot o‘chiriladi

  • Profil: ism, telefon, email, shahar, profil rasmi.
  • Servis markaz profili, galereya va yuklangan servis fotolari.
  • Siz qo‘shgan avtomobillar, servis yozuvlari va izohlar.

Qanday ma’lumot saqlanishi mumkin

  • Buxgalteriya/qonuniy talablar uchun to‘lov va obuna yozuvlari 5 yilgacha saqlanadi, so‘ng o‘chiriladi.
  • Sizni aniqlay olmaydigan anonim, umumlashtirilgan statistika.
